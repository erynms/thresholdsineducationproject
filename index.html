Welcome

This website presents an academic exploration of the Thresholds special issue on Generative AI in higher education. Through eight articles, this issue examines how writing and composition classrooms are navigating the pedagogical crossroads created by generative AI technologies.

Rather than advocating for or against AI adoption, this collection presents pragmatic, evidence-based approaches grounded in actual classroom experiences. The articles move beyond speculation to offer frameworks, findings, and frameworks for critical engagement with generative AI tools.

At the Crossroads

The rapid emergence of generative AI has created what the editors call "a giant public experiment no one asked for." This special issue positions educators as uniquely positioned to lead thoughtful engagement with these technologies (Watkins & Monroe, 2025, p. 3), exploring how to preserve core educational values while adapting to new technological realities.

About Thresholds

Thresholds in Education is an academic journal dedicated to research and practice in educational contexts. This special issue brings together scholars from composition, rhetoric, writing studies, and educational technology to examine one of the most pressing challenges facing contemporary education.
