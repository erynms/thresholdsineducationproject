Generative AI in Education: Rethinking Learning, Assessment & Student Agency for the AI Era

D.M.N. Uanachain & L.I. Aouad

Uanachain, D.M.N., & Aouad, L.I. (2025). Generative AI in education: Rethinking learning, assessment & student agency for the AI era. Thresholds in Education, 48(1), 111-132.

Summary

Uanachain and Aouad present a framework for integrating GenAI into curriculum for student use and pedagogical assessment practices in a unified approach. They acknowledge the broad potential for what they call cognitive atrophy from overuse of GenAI tools and believe that their framework contributes to three core values: discouraging academic dishonesty, protecting student data privacy, and supporting the closing of the digital divide.

Proposing the adaptation of Educational Digital Gradual Immersion Model for Education, a framework developed by Sanbria, the authors argue that the process of using digital tools to produce physical products encourages active learning and skill development. Products and processes are assessed as a whole, encouraging whole effort and potentially revealing misuse of GenAI tools.

The application of Generativism, an educational theory proposed by Pratschke, which presents education as a collaborative effort between the student and GenAI, is the key to the authors' framework.

Key Contributions

  • Unified framework for curriculum integration and assessment
  • Adaptation of the Educational Digital Gradual Immersion Model
  • Application of Generativism educational theory
  • Focus on preventing cognitive atrophy through thoughtful design
  • Attention to academic integrity, data privacy, and digital equity
  • Holistic assessment approach evaluating both products and processes

Implications

This article provides a comprehensive theoretical and practical framework for educators seeking to integrate AI thoughtfully. By attending to concerns about cognitive atrophy, academic integrity, and equity, the authors address many of the most pressing worries about AI in education while still embracing its potential benefits.
