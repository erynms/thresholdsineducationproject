Critiquing ChatGPT Compositions: Collaborative Annotation as an Approach to Enhancing Students' Metalinguistic Awareness of AI-Generated Writing

R. Li

Li, R. (2025). Critiquing ChatGPT compositions: Collaborative annotation as an approach to enhancing students' metalinguistic awareness of AI-generated writing. Thresholds in Education, 48(1), 7-24.

Summary

Li introduces a collaborative annotation activity that invites students to closely examine essays generated by ChatGPT, focusing specifically on criteria such as specificity and complexity. The article argues that through this process, students develop increased metalinguistic awareness by identifying moments where AI-generated text lacks depth, nuance, or rhetorical subtlety.

By reflecting on how such writing could be revised to be more specific or complex, students become more agentive, shifting from passive users of AI output to critics of it. Li contends that this pedagogical design fosters dialogic human-AI interaction and reframes writing instruction in the age of generative tools.

This study engages with AI rhetorics by treating AI-generated writing not merely as a tool or threat but as a rhetorical artifact to be critiqued. It shows how big-data-driven language models mediate writing processes, authorship, and student agency.

Key Contributions

  • Collaborative annotation as a pedagogical method for AI literacy
  • Development of metalinguistic awareness through AI critique
  • Shift from passive AI users to active critics
  • Framework for dialogic human-AI interaction
  • AI-generated writing as rhetorical artifact for study
