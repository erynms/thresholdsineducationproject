Introduction: Pedagogical Crossroads: Higher Education in the Age of Generative AI

M. Watkins & S. Monroe

Watkins, M. & Monroe, S. (2025). Introduction: Pedagogical crossroads: Higher education in the age of generative AI. Thresholds in Education, 48(1), 1-6

Summary

The editors frame this special issue by acknowledging the real and material challenges that large language model application interfaces bring to the teaching profession, particularly for composition-based classrooms. Each essay addresses obstacles and opportunities that generative AI brings to education, offering encouragement and specific ideas for engaging with interfaces that teachers cannot avoid.

Watkins and Monroe argue that generative AI brings new affordances to teaching, and only through collaboration and adaptation can we reach an equilibrium that works for both teachers and students. This introduction establishes the collection's central premise: educators stand at a pedagogical crossroads where the path forward requires thoughtful engagement rather than resistance or uncritical adoption.

Key Contributions

  • Frames generative AI as unavoidable reality requiring thoughtful engagement
  • Establishes "pedagogical crossroads" metaphor for current moment in education
  • Acknowledges material challenges while highlighting new affordances
  • Calls for collaboration and adaptation over resistance
  • Positions the special issue as practical resource for educators
